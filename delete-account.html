Nutmeg

Delete your Nutmeg account

If you use Nutmeg on your own without an account, your data is stored only on your phone, and deleting the app removes it. If you created an account to share with a household, you can delete it in two ways.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nutmeg and go to Household settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email brianjaqua@proton.me from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address, and password.
  • Your household membership.
  • If you are the last member of your household: the household, all its recipes, photos, and meal plans.
  • If other members remain, the recipes and photos you added stay with the household for them.

What may be kept

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where we are legally required to keep it. Backups are cleared within 30 days.
