Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]

Nutmeg ("the app") is made by Northbound Press ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

Using the app on your own (no account)

You can use Nutmeg without an account. Your recipes, photos, and meal plan are then stored only on your phone. We don't receive or store any of it. Deleting the app deletes that data. A small banner ad may still appear; see Advertising below.

What we collect when you share with a household

You only create an account if you choose to share with a household. When you share for the first time, the recipes, photos, and meal plan already on your phone are uploaded to that household. From then on we collect:

  • Account info: your email address and a password (the password is stored hashed by our sign-in provider; we never see it).
  • Household info: the household name, its members' emails, and its invite code.
  • Content you add: recipes (titles, ingredients, steps, tags, servings), recipe photos, meal-plan entries, and grocery-list items, plus which member added them.
  • Photos: only the images you choose from your camera or library to attach to a recipe. We do not access the rest of your photo library.

When you import a recipe from a link, your phone fetches that web page directly; we don't receive the link.

We do not collect your contacts or precise location. We do not sell your data.

Advertising

The app is supported by one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob (a third party). AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising ID / IDFA, if you allow it
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with ads

Google explains how it uses data from partner sites and apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we show Google's consent form (UMP) before personalized ads.
  • On iPhone, we show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • If you decline, you still see ads, but they are non-personalized. Your choice does not change any features.

You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings:

  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy / Security → Ads), then reset or delete the advertising ID

You can remove the banner forever with an optional one-time purchase; see In-app purchases below.

In-app purchases

All features are free. The only purchase is an optional one-time "Buy me a cup of coffee" purchase that removes ads forever (not a subscription).

The purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your card details. Purchase status is stored on your device and can be restored with Restore Purchases, for example after you get a new phone.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your recipes and meal plan to the members of your household, and sync changes between them live.
  • To show a banner ad (through Google AdMob), unless you have removed ads.
  • To respond if you contact us.

Who can see your data

  • Members of your household can see and edit the household's recipes, photos, and meal plan, and see the member list.
  • Our service provider: household account and content data is stored and processed by Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage) on our behalf. See https://supabase.com/privacy.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may collect and use the advertising data described above. See Google's policy linked in Advertising.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your shared data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Household settings → Delete account.
  • Without the app: request deletion at https://district375.github.io/delete-account.html or email brianjaqua@proton.me.

When you delete your account, we delete your account, email, and membership. Recipes and photos you added to a household that still has other members stay with that household, so the remaining members keep them. If you are the last member, the whole household and its content are deleted. The same happens if the last member leaves a household. The copy of your recipes, photos, and meal plan on your phone stays on your phone. Deletion is completed within 30 days, including backups.

Deleting your account does not automatically remove ads or delete advertising data held by Google. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone settings, as described above.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each household can only access its own data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Ad content is limited to a family-appropriate rating (G in AdMob).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
